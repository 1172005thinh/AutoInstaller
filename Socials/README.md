# SOCIALS

This directory includes all the social-app installers.

- **Discord**
- **Zalo**

## DISCORD

The installers installs a fresh `Discord` desktop app and disable `automatic startup`. You may launch the app once to login and let's it automatically handle the rest.

## ZALO

The installer installs `Zalo` with `default configuration`. You may launch the app once to login, sync chat history, then modify it to your preference.
